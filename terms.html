What the calculator is

WonBack's calculators are free estimating tools. They apply published rules — the ₩15,000 minimum purchase, the instant-refund limits, and a refund operator's tier table — to amounts you enter, and show the result in your browser.

What it is not

The result is an estimate, not a quotation, a guarantee or tax advice. The refund you actually receive is decided by the operator that processes your receipt and by customs at departure, and it can differ from the estimate because of the operator's own table, currency handling, an item being ineligible, or a rule change after the date shown on the page. Always check the slip the shop gives you.

Nothing here creates a professional or advisory relationship, and the site is not affiliated with any refund operator, retailer, airline or government body.
