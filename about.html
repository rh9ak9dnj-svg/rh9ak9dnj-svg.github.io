About WonBack

Last updated 27 November 2026

WonBack is a small independent site built in Seoul. It makes free calculators for the money questions travellers ask in Korea — starting with the tourist VAT refund, where the official rules are clear but the arithmetic is not: a minimum per receipt, two separate instant-refund limits, and refund amounts that move in tiers instead of a percentage.

How the numbers are checked

Every rule on the calculator comes from a primary or official source, and the page states the date it was checked:

  • Limits and eligibility come from the presidential decree on VAT and individual consumption tax for foreign tourists, and from VisitKorea's official refund guide.
  • Refund amounts use Global Tax Free's published tier table, which is the most complete table operators make public. Other operators publish their own, so the page says plainly that the figure is an estimate and that the operator decides.
  • Arithmetic is tested before every change: the tier table is checked for gaps and for refunds that fail to rise, the caps are checked at their exact boundaries, and formatted or negative input is checked for silent errors.

What this site does not do

  • It does not process refunds, take passport details or ask for an account.
  • It does not send what you type anywhere. The calculation runs in your browser, and the only thing stored is your preferred dollar rate.
  • It does not replace the operator's slip or a customs officer's decision. If the two disagree, they are right.

Corrections

Rules change — both instant-refund limits doubled on 1 January 2024, and the minimum purchase was lowered to ₩15,000. If you find a figure that no longer matches the official source, write to rh9ak9dnj@gmail.com with the page and the source, and it will be checked and dated.
