Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use WonBack. The short version: the calculator runs in your browser, the numbers you type are not sent to us, and there is no account to create.

What you type stays in your browser

Receipt amounts and refund methods are used only to draw the result on your screen. They are not transmitted, logged or stored. Closing or reloading the page clears them.

One preference is saved in your browser's local storage: the dollar exchange rate, so you do not have to re-enter it. It holds only a number and never leaves your device. Clearing site data removes it. If your browser blocks storage, the calculator still works.

Server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider, which processes ordinary request data — IP address, requested page, time, browser type, referrer — to deliver the page and protect the service from abuse. We do not build profiles from it or combine it with anything else.

Advertising

Pages may show advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices in the EU.
  • Google's own explanation is in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Ad providers may set cookies when an ad loads; that happens under their policies, not ours. If you are in a region that requires consent for advertising cookies, a consent message from the ad provider appears before those cookies are set.

Affiliate links

Some links to shops and booking services are affiliate links. When you click one, the partner may set a cookie recording that the visit came from this site, so a later purchase can be credited. That cookie is set by the partner and covered by its privacy policy. It does not change the price you pay, and it does not give us your name, payment details or address.

What we never collect

  • No account, name, email or phone number is requested to use the tools.
  • No passport, receipt image or payment detail is uploaded.
  • No third-party analytics or session-recording script runs on this site.

Children

The site is aimed at adults planning purchases and travel, and is not directed at children under 14.

Your choices and questions

You can clear local storage, block cookies, or use your browser's private mode at any time; the calculator keeps working. For any question about this policy, or to ask what is stored, write to rh9ak9dnj@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes — a new ad network, a new kind of data — will be described here before they take effect.
